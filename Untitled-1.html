Sanctions

All sanctions must remain confidential.

The staff will not announce when a player is sanctioned to avoid judgment or public humiliation - that is not what Ruthless Roleplay is about.

Only the parties involved will have knowledge of any sanctions. Disclosing sanctions to others will result in the disclosing player being sanctioned as well.

The staff does not want to issue sanctions lightly. However, to maintain the type of community we aim to build, we enforce a zero-tolerance policy for rule-breaking:

Sanctions:

  1. 1st Offense: 24-Hour Ban
  2. 2nd Offense: 48-Hour Ban
  3. 3rd Offense: 1-Week Ban
  4. 4th Offense: Permanent Ban

Appeals:

Players may appeal sanctions. Depending on the severity of the offense, the staff reserves the right to lift or uphold a ban at their discretion.

Community Conduct

Respect and Safe Space

We are dedicated to maintaining a safe and welcoming space where players can express their creativity through roleplay. All members are expected to treat one another with respect at all times. Hate speech, harassment, or any form of abusive or discriminatory language will not be tolerated, even within a roleplay scenario. Respect everyone, contribute positively, and help keep Ruthless Roleplay an enjoyable and fun community.

Zero Tolerance to Bullying or Harassing

Bullying, discrimination, or harassment of any kind is not tolerated in our community. Members must never engage in communication or behavior that is predatory, threatening, intimidating, lewd, demeaning, derogatory, or intended to cause discomfort or disrupt another player’s experience. Insults or verbal attacks outside of “in-character roleplay” are strictly prohibited.

Cheating and Exploits

Cheating, hacking, the use of unauthorized third-party programs or scripts, exploiting bugs, or engaging in any activity that provides an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited. Players suspected of hacking or exploiting may have their access temporarily suspended while an investigation is conducted. If you discover a glitch or exploit, you are required to report it by submitting a ticket.

Firestarting

Disrespecting roleplayers or staff, intentionally provoking others, or engaging in heated arguments in public chat channels will not be tolerated. If you have an issue with another member, address it privately or submit a report rather than creating unnecessary conflict. Remember that as a member of Ruthless Roleplay, you represent the community even when out of character, and toxic behavior in public spaces will not be accepted.

Banned Content

Content related to the following topics is strictly prohibited and may result in a permanent community ban. This restriction applies to all forms of content, including usernames, avatars, profile details, and any links that direct to such material:

  • Nudity or sexually explicit content
  • Rape or sexual assault
  • Child abuse or pedophilia
  • Bestiality or necrophilia
  • Slavery or human trafficking
  • Images depicting bodily fluids or excrement
  • Gore, mutilation, or graphic violence
  • Suicide or any form of self-harm
  • Symbols associated with hate groups
  • Terrorism, genocide, or real-world tragedies

Admin Discretion and Rule Enforcement

Administration has the final say on what is considered a breach of the rules, unacceptable behavior, or actions that create a negative experience for others. Any conduct that harms the roleplaying environment or goes against the community’s standards may result in disciplinary action at the discretion of staff.

General Rules

Low Quality Roleplay (LQRP)

Low Quality Roleplay (LQRP) refers to actions that break immersion, ignore character development, or force outcomes without proper narrative justification. Examples include, but are not limited to:

  • Mindless or random player killings
  • Forced character deaths without consent
  • Excessive metagaming or powergaming
  • Disruptive actions that do not contribute to the story

LQRP is taken seriously. Players engaging in such behavior may receive sanctions according to the severity and frequency of the offenses. Always ensure your actions serve the story and respect the RP experience of others.

MetaGaming

Metagaming, or the use of information your character has not learned through roleplay, will not be tolerated. Players must not use knowledge gained outside of character—such as Discord, streams, or other sources—in a way that gives their character an unfair advantage. You may only act on information that your character has directly learned through in-character interactions.

Powergaming

Powergaming in any form is strictly prohibited. Powergaming is defined as using game mechanics, external information, or roleplay concepts in a way that forces actions on others or gives your character an unfair or unrealistic advantage.

Combat Logging

Combat logging, the act of disconnecting or leaving the server to avoid in-game combat consequences, is now allowed on Ruthless Roleplay. Players may disconnect during combat without penalty, though it is encouraged to roleplay outcomes whenever possible to maintain immersion.

Value of Life

Players must always value their own life and the lives of other characters, regardless of gameplay mechanics that allow respawning or returning after being downed. Characters are expected to act realistically in situations that could result in injury or death, and should never behave in a way that disregards their life or survival.

While your character’s personality may be brave, arrogant, or even condescending in tone, you must still roleplay as though they want to live when faced with danger or life threatening situation.

During car chases, players must avoid performing unrealistic stunts or jumps.

In shootouts, where law enforcement is present, the priority should be to escape rather than engage in a gunfight. Resorting to lethal force against police should only happen as a last option when no other alternatives remain.

Roleplay Over Gameplay

Players must never prioritize gameplay mechanics or “winning” over roleplay. Actions that undermine roleplay for the sake of personal gain or advantage are not permitted. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Excessively focusing on character, creature, or equipment stats
  • Repeatedly tailoring builds or stats only for the current task
  • Performing tasks or using skills that do not fit your character’s role
  • Constructing buildings with no roleplay purpose
  • Taming or breeding creatures with no intent to trade or sell
  • Choosing gameplay actions or solo play over roleplay interaction
  • Collecting wealth, resources, or items without roleplay context
  • Attacking players without narrative or sufficient roleplay interaction

Erotic Roleplay (ERP)

While Ruthless Roleplay does not encourage erotic roleplay (ERP), in the event that such roleplay occurs within the server, it is considered a regulated activity and must be approached with the utmost sensitivity. Consent from all parties is mandatory, and ERP may not take place without clear agreement. Furthermore, recording, streaming, or distributing ERP is strictly prohibited. Any attempt to capture, share, or disregard the consent requirement will be treated as a serious violation of community standards. Members are expected to respect these boundaries at all times to preserve a safe and comfortable environment for everyone.

New Life Rule

The New Life Rule (NLR) means that when your character dies during roleplay and you needed to respawn, they are not considered permanently dead unless you choose to “permanently kill” your character. Also, you may not return to the location of your death and you must forget all events leading up to your death unless revived by medical personnel. This rule only applies if revival is done by Player Medical Staff, not AI Medical Staff. Headshots or gunshot wounds to the head, extreme head trauma will not be treated as amnesia, and “amnesia roleplay” is not permitted. Ruthless Roleplay aims to ensure that all storylines are progressive and meaningful, and amnesia roleplay undermines this standard.

Use of Voice Changer

The use of a voice changer is allowed under the following conditions:

  • Your voice must still be audible; excessively high-pitched or low-pitched voices are not allowed.
  • The voice changer cannot be used solely to hide your identity.
  • If you create a character with a specific voice, you must use that voice consistently throughout the roleplay for that character.

Character Rules

Character Creation

Character creation should be thoughtful and immersive. Names must be realistic whenever possible and may not include trolling elements, double meanings, or anything inappropriate. Ruthless Roleplay reserves the right to deny applications that do not meet these standards. While certain “nicknames” may be developed and used in-game once your character has been established, the name submitted in your application must always follow community guidelines.

Gender Sensitivity

Gender and sexuality must always be respected in roleplay. Characters should be treated according to how they are presented in-game, regardless of the player’s real-life voice or identity. For example, if a player is roleplaying a female character, that character should be treated and acknowledged as a woman, even if the player’s voice suggests otherwise. Questioning or mocking a character’s gender based on voice is not allowed.

Voice modification software (“mentos”) is permitted, but it must be properly calibrated to remain clear and understandable so that immersion and interaction are not disrupted.

Voice Recognition is Allowed

Players must be aware that roleplay interactions may include voice identification. Wearing masks or other disguises does not prevent recognition through speech or voice patterns. Players are not allowed to use third-party software or tools to alter their voice in order to bypass identification. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Attempting to hide your identity while speaking in-character
  • Masks or costumes do not prevent other players from recognizing your voice
  • Using voice modulation or software to disguise your speech is not allowed
  • If you are already using voice modulation software, you cannot alter it further to bypass identification
  • Claiming untraceable status despite being identifiable by voice
  • Exploiting voice anonymity to gain unfair advantage or disrupt roleplay

Conflict Encounters

Conflict & Player Interaction

Ruthless Roleplay is not a PvP server. While player killings can occur as part of immersive roleplay, they should never be the default solution to conflicts. Our goal is to create a server where interactions, tensions, and rivalries are story-driven, not dominated by constant combat. Every conflict should serve the narrative of your character and the broader RP world.

Player killings must always be justified in-character. Mindlessly killing other players or using violence as a shortcut to solve disagreements is strictly prohibited. Any killings that do not have a deep meaning or proper build-up will be considered low-quality roleplay and may result in sanctions. This applies regardless of whether your character is a criminal, a law-abiding citizen, or someone with personal grudges. Random, unmotivated killings ruin immersion and disrupt the RP experience for everyone.

Forced Character Kill: Forcing the death of another character is not allowed. Any in-character killing must have the clear consent of all involved parties where applicable. Players cannot arbitrarily end another player’s character without agreement.

Decapitation: Decapitation is now allowed in-game as part of immersive roleplay. All instances must be justified in-character and follow the same standards of consent and story relevance.

Conflict is an opportunity to develop your character’s story, relationships, and motivations. Before resorting to lethal force, consider alternatives such as:

  • Negotiation or intimidation
  • Deception or strategy
  • Collaboration with allies or factions
  • Non-lethal consequences (injuries, captures, public humiliation)

Every action in conflict has in-character consequences. If your character kills, threatens, or harms another, expect realistic responses from law enforcement, other players, or storylines. Ignoring consequences or attempting to bypass RP repercussions is considered Fail RP and may result in sanctions.

Being creative in conflict not only enhances your character’s journey but also allows others to respond in interesting, realistic ways, resulting in richer and more engaging RP. Conflict should enhance the RP experience for all involved, not dominate it or force outcomes. Work with other players to ensure that every confrontation contributes meaningfully to the story.

REMINDER: The goal is immersive, evolving narratives, not a constant “win at all costs” mentality. Conflict is a tool for storytelling, not a free-for-all. Use it to enrich your character’s journey, develop relationships, and create memorable stories. Be patient, creative, and always respect the RP experience of others.

Any player killings that lack deep meaning, proper build-up, or clear in-character justification will be considered low-quality roleplay and may result in sanctions, including bans. All killings must serve the story and enhance the immersive RP experience.

Whitelisted Jobs / Players

Certain jobs on Ruthless Roleplay are whitelisted, meaning that they are restricted to players who have been approved by staff to take on these roles. Whitelisted jobs are designed to maintain quality roleplay and ensure that players in key roles understand the responsibilities and expectations involved.

Professionalism

Players in whitelisted roles are expected to act professionally and realistically at all times.

Roleplay Standards

Whitelisted jobs must follow server rules and engage in immersive, story-driven roleplay.

No Exploitation

Whitelisted jobs must not be used to gain unfair advantages, metagame, powergame, or harass other players.

Reminder

Whitelisted roles are a privilege, not a right. Players are expected to uphold the integrity of the role, contribute to immersive roleplay, and act as examples for the community.

No Dirty Whitelisted

Players in whitelisted jobs are strictly prohibited from engaging in “dirty” activities that give any player or group, especially criminal organizations, an unfair advantage. Abuse of whitelisted roles undermines the integrity of the server and will not be tolerated.

While players may roleplay arrogant, rude, or confrontational characters, they must accept the consequences of their actions in-character. Your character’s job does not grant immunity from legal, social, or other in-character consequences.

If your character’s arrogance or attitude leads to conflict, fights, or other in-character situations, you are required to play it out realistically and follow proper roleplay standards.

EMS Roleplay

When receiving EMS roleplay, please be patient and respect the process. Medical procedures take time, and the steps needed depend on the type and severity of the injury. Players must roleplay their injuries and treatment realistically — you cannot walk away immediately after being treated as if nothing happened.

All roleplay provided by EMS staff or players must be respected. Do not interrupt, ignore, or dismiss the treatment process. Remember that EMS RP may involve multiple steps, dialogue, and actions, and this is essential to creating a believable and immersive experience for everyone involved.

Law Enforcement Roleplay

When interacting with law enforcement (LEO) in RP, players must respect the process and follow realistic roleplay. Officers may take time to investigate, question, or detain individuals depending on the situation. All actions taken by law enforcement must be respected, and players are expected to respond in-character according to the scenario.

Players cannot ignore, bypass, or disrupt the actions of law enforcement for convenience or personal gain. Arrests, searches, investigations, and other procedures are part of creating a believable and immersive RP experience, and everyone involved must participate cooperatively and realistically.

When LEOs find themselves in a life-threatening situation, they have the right to exert the appropriate level of force depending on the circumstances. So, if you think you can just brandish a weapon or commit crimes without consequence, don’t be surprised if LEOs take action, especially if they have probable cause. Remember, if you’re living a life of crime, you must be prepared to face the consequences.

Department of Justice (DOJ) Roleplay

Players interacting with the Department of Justice (DOJ) must respect all legal procedures and follow realistic roleplay. This includes investigations, court proceedings, prosecutions, and any other in-character legal actions. All actions carried out by DOJ staff or players must be respected, and participants are expected to respond in-character according to the scenario.

DOJ members are tasked with maintaining law, order, and fairness within the server. Players cannot ignore, bypass, or disrupt DOJ actions for convenience or personal gain. All legal processes should be treated as part of immersive, story-driven roleplay, and everyone involved must participate cooperatively and realistically.

When DOJ members are carrying out investigations, arrests, or prosecutions, they have the right to use their authority appropriately according to the situation. Players who engage in criminal activity or attempt to obstruct justice must be prepared to face legal consequences, including trials, sentencing, and other in-character outcomes.

Do not avoid court hearings or court-related RP — this is half the fun and an important part of your character’s storyline. Don’t just skip town; allow the RP to develop and let your character face the consequences of their actions.

Illegal Activities

Illegal Activities Team Limit

All major illegal activities involving robberies — such as ATM robbery, house robbery, store robbery, etc. — must begin with only 4 players. Limiting team sizes ensures balanced, immersive roleplay and prevents large-scale abuse or disruption of the RP experience.

Petty or small-time heists like carjacking, car chopping, and drug selling can be done alone at your own risk with a maximum amount of 4 players allowed.

Additional members may only join the group once the robbery has escalated into a pursuit that has already been ongoing for a significant period of time (around 15–20 minutes). At that stage, the group may call for assistance using any strategy. However, engaging in a gunfight should always be treated as the last option, not the first.

Robbing / Hold-Ups

Robbing or holding up players is not permitted under any circumstances. In the event that you find yourself in a roleplay where you are up against a rival group or player and find that robbing them as a necessity for the preservation of your own life, you can rob them and take their weapon for your own safety but this conflict must have a significant story build up leading to this. The said weapon may or may not be returned to the player that was robbed.

Hostage Taking

All hostage situations must be planned in advance. You cannot take someone hostage who is a friend, ally, or directly affiliated with you; hostages must be sought out realistically as part of the robbery or criminal scenario.

If you take a hostage, you must roleplay it properly — for example, making the hostage kneel, comply with commands, or pointing a weapon. Even with a hostage, there are risks involved, so ensure you have leverage and a believable plan. Players are not allowed to rob or kill the hostage. If a hostage possesses a deadly weapon, it may be taken for your own safety but must be returned once the scenario concludes.

Players are not allowed to take someone hostage or demand a ransom unless it is directly related to a robbery scenario. Hostage situations must always be contextual, realistic, and part of an immersive roleplay scenario. Random or unrelated hostage situations are prohibited and may result in sanctions.

When taking hostages, the number of demands you can make depends on how many hostages you have:

  • 1 hostage: 2 demands
  • 2 hostages: 3 demands
  • 3 or more hostages: 3 demands

NOTE: NPCs are not considered as hostages by any means.

Allowed Demands

  • Free passage
  • No helicopters
  • No spike strips
  • No GPS trackers
  • Cash ransom up to $50K regardless of the number of hostages

Note: Money demands must remain on the player at all times; it cannot be stored in gloveboxes, trunks, or deposited into the bank.

Prohibited / Unreasonable Demands

  • Requiring all law enforcement to not be in their vehicles before a chase begins
  • Requesting a specific person to take primary control in the chase
  • Any form of illegal items such as marked money or items that can be used to commit a crime

Demands will be deemed void if the chase falls under any of the following categories:

  • Law enforcement deems that the pursuit has caused multiple government property damages
  • The chase has already exceeded an extended period of time (10 minutes)
  • The one being pursued has run over a civilian (player or NPC)
  • The one being pursued has declared any immediate threat verbally, or if any back-up/allies get involved in the chase

All hostage RP must be planned, realistic, and immersive, following these guidelines to ensure a balanced and engaging experience for everyone involved.

Vehicle Restrictions

All robbery-related pursuits must use vehicles that meet the following requirements for a fair and balanced chase:

  • Vehicles must have at least 4 doors / 4 seats.
  • Vehicles must not exceed a maximum speed of 110 mph to match law enforcement vehicle capabilities.
  • Unrealistic or overpowered vehicles for pursuits are strictly prohibited.

Unrealistic Vehicle Jumps

Any unrealistic or extreme vehicle jumps are strictly prohibited. Attempting jumps that defy the game’s physics or could be considered over-the-top is not allowed. Such actions may also be classified as No-Fail/Fail RP (NFL) and can result in sanctions. All stunts and jumps must be plausible and roleplayed realistically.

Costume Restrictions

Players are free to wear any outfit or costume they like, except for full head masks that cover the entire head and alter the player’s head size. All other costumes are allowed, but any outfit that creates unrealistic proportions or obstructs gameplay may be prohibited.

Cop Baiting

Cop baiting is strictly prohibited. Forcing law enforcement into a chase over a minor situation, such as a simple traffic stop, is not allowed. You may attempt to flee only if there is a justified reason — for example, an active warrant or knowingly driving a vehicle used in the commission of a crime. All interactions with law enforcement should develop naturally through roleplay, not through unrealistic or disruptive behavior.

Rob & Go

Every robbery or illegal activity must be carried out with proper roleplay, meaning it should be planned and executed with thought and purpose. For some situations, hostages are required for high-risk robberies, and there must be clear in-character actions such as demands, negotiations, and escape planning. Once a robbery or heist has been triggered, despite if the attempt has failed, you must see it through and wait for Law Enforcement players to arrive before moving forward. Meaning - quick grab-and-dash tactics that skip realistic roleplay are not permitted.

Pocket Wipe

Pocket wiping is strictly prohibited at Ruthless Roleplay. You are not allowed to loot a downed player without proper roleplay engagement — whether civilian to civilian, civilian to gang (and vice versa), or criminals to law enforcement.

You are only allowed to loot the following illegal items from a downed player:

  • Drugs
  • Illegal weapons and ammunition
  • Melee weapons
  • Illegal-related items (e.g., marked bills, illegal or robbery items, stolen goods)

Items such as phones, tablets, IDs, clean money, PD or EMS equipment, and other personal belongings are strictly forbidden to take under any circumstances.

Remember, everything in Ruthless Roleplay is continuous roleplay — your actions have lasting consequences. If you kill or seriously injure someone and they receive medical treatment, their character will remember the incident and may pursue in-game repercussions. However, if the individual chooses to respawn instead of receiving treatment, their character will not remember the events that led to their injuries.

Palit Ulo

Palit Ulo is strictly prohibited in Ruthless Roleplay. Likewise, you cannot have your group take a hostage and demand your freedom in exchange. Being a criminal means accepting the risks and consequences of your actions. If you are caught, then you are caught — going to jail is part of the roleplay experience and must be respected at all times.

Gang Rules

Gangs and Criminal Organizations

At Ruthless Roleplay, we believe gangs and criminal organizations should have depth and purpose. It’s not just about mindless crimes or random gang wars — successful roleplay comes from creating a compelling storyline behind your group. We encourage gangs to develop rich histories, motivations, and objectives that make their actions meaningful and immersive within the world of Los Santos.

Background Stories

Every gang must have a storyline behind it. Origin stories give your group depth, guide its direction, and make roleplay more immersive. You can’t just say, “Let’s form a gang,” and have it appear out of nowhere. There must be a clear reason for how the gang began, and the individual storylines of its members should interact in meaningful ways.

Remember: gangs are not mass-recruiting machines. At Ruthless Roleplay, engagement between members and prospective recruits should be deliberate and significant, creating connections that enhance the overall story.

Gang Identity and Representation

If you and your friends decide to form a gang, you are required to clearly declare your affiliation both verbally and visually. Visually means through clothing — this could be a vest, bandana, or even a full suit to capture a specific vibe, such as a mafia aesthetic. Whatever the style, your gang or criminal organization must be easily identifiable and remain consistent with its lore.

For example, if your group is meant to be a street gang, members are expected to dress, behave, and roleplay in a way that reflects that identity.

At Ruthless Roleplay, gangs can be recognized not only by their colors or clothing but also by the areas they frequent or consider their territory. Your gang’s identity can be tied to a specific barrio or neighborhood — for example, a gang wearing blue could be based in Vagos, or another group could represent Jamestown while sporting blue. Territory and location help reinforce your gang’s presence in the world and make roleplay more immersive.

Gang Color Representation and Color Wars

Gang colors can help identify your group, but no gang may claim a color as exclusively their own. For this reason, gang-related violence over colors is strictly prohibited, and “color wars” are not allowed. Roleplay should focus on meaningful interactions and story-driven conflicts, not disputes over clothing or hues.

Gang Wars

Gang wars may only occur when there is a legitimate reason for conflict. Examples of valid reasons include betrayal, snitching, or major territorial disputes. Petty reasons — such as trash-talking or personal grudges — are not sufficient to justify a war.

Gang wars are only permitted once both gang leaders agree and formally declare war. The conflict begins one hour after the declaration and is limited to three (3) real-life days. During this time, each gang is allowed one opportunity per day to engage in gunplay.

In terms of weapons, gangs are allowed to use any weapon in their arsenal. However, law enforcement reserves the right to intervene whenever necessary.

Officers may apprehend individuals during gang wars, though they will provide reasonable time to disperse before taking action.

It is your responsibility as gang members that when you start hearing sirens, you should gather all your fallen gang members and vacate the area. Any gang member left behind will be directly brought to law enforcement custody.

On the fourth day, gang leaders will coordinate one final engagement, after which both parties must enter a truce. Immediate re-engagement or forced conflicts with other gangs is not allowed — roleplay is about meaningful storytelling, not constant gunfights.

Gang war details, including time, location, and number of participants, must be pre-determined and agreed upon by both gang leaders. For example, if a war is scheduled for 1 PM, leaders will agree on a specific location and headcount to ensure balanced and fair engagements.

Gang Alliances

Gang alliances are not allowed during gang wars. You may conduct business with other gangs, such as trades or purchases, but if a war is declared, affiliated gangs cannot intervene.

If two gangs that have a friendly relationship both find themselves at war with the same gang, the respective gang leaders must coordinate and discuss the arrangements. Planning and communication are key — this ensures fairness and proper roleplay.
